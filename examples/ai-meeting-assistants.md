## Method and run stats

1. **Competitors** come from comparison and "best of" articles, ranked by how many independent sources mention them, and kept only if their homepage is a product in this market.
2. **Features and pricing** come from each company's own pages. Every feature cell and price must be found in the page text, or it is left out.
3. **Complaints** are extracted from Hacker News comments and low-rated App Store reviews. Each one quotes the customer word for word; quotes that can't be found in the source are dropped.
4. **Pain points** are clusters of similar complaints, each marked a product failure or a need. For needs, coverage asks whether each competitor's own site claims to address the problem.
5. **Gaps** are scored in code, never by the LLM. A pain point is ranked only with at least 5 complaints from 3 documents about 2 companies, and at most 3 complaints per document count. Market gaps also need 2 source types.
6. **Verification:** every quote shown under a gap is checked word for word against its source and judged by an LLM for whether it supports the gap.

- Complaints: App Store 172, Hacker News 57
- Verification: 9/9 ranked pain points verified; 55/61 quotes passed (90%)
- Agents: 1 discovery rounds, 1 rounds of follow-up research

  - 'Forced google or microsoft login' weak 6.5 → 'Forced google or microsoft login' weak 6.5 (+0 complaints), unchanged
